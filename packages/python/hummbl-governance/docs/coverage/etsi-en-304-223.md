## Boundary disclaimer

HUMMBL is **not** an ETSI member deliverable, **not** a national standards body, and **not** a certified AI product under this EN. EN 304 223 is a **baseline cyber security** European Standard for AI models and systems across five lifecycle phases. It is addressed to stakeholders in the AI supply chain (EN Introduction / clause 4 Stakeholders). HUMMBL is an in-process governance library.

**National endorsement (from the EN Foreword, National transposition dates):** adoption 8 December 2025; latest announcement (doa) 31 March 2026; latest publication of a new National Standard or endorsement (dop/e) **30 September 2026**; withdrawal of conflicting National Standards (dow) 30 September 2026.

**Complements** JTC 21 [`prEN 18282`](./cen-clc-jtc21.md) (cybersecurity specifications for AI systems; unpublished draft in that file). **Do not collapse them.** EN 304 223 is an ETSI EN with published principle text; prEN 18282 is a CEN-CENELEC draft without public clauses in this corpus.

**No public “fulfills EN 304 223.”** Overlap is Partial at most. No row in this file is ✅.

## Principle-ID honesty

**Principle IDs and titles are copied from EN 304 223 V2.1.1 clause 5** (Contents + clause headings). No additional principle numbers are invented. Completeness for the EN = the **13 principles** plus explicit programme / non-collapse rows. Sub-provisions under each principle (the SHALL/SHOULD sentences inside 5.1.1–5.5.1) are **not** exploded into extra IDs.

Five lifecycle phases named in the EN Introduction and clause 5 headings: Secure Design, Secure Development, Secure Deployment, Secure Maintenance, Secure End of Life. The EN NOTE maps those phases to ISO/IEC 22989 lifecycle stages; that mapping is the EN's, not a HUMMBL claim. See [`iso-iec-22989.md`](./iso-iec-22989.md) (last reviewed 2026-05-14 / v0.8.0 — not upgraded here).
