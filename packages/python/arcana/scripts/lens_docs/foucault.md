### 1. Algorithmic HR Screening Bias

In 2023, a multinational tech firm deployed an AI tool to auto-reject resumes lacking specific coding keywords from its historical dataset. The system learned from past hiring decisions where male candidates and those with specific engineering backgrounds were systematically favored. Consequently, applicants with non-linear career paths or gaps for caregiving were disproportionately filtered out before a human ever saw their application. This process creates a 'docile' applicant who self-corrects their resume to match the algorithm's invisible norm, while the firm claims objective efficiency.

**What foucault sees:** The algorithm acts as a regime of truth that produces a new subjectivity by defining 'competence' through historical data, thereby excluding forms of knowledge that do not align with existing power structures.

**What foucault misses:** The tangible economic hardship and immediate stress caused to the thousands of applicants who face genuine automation without any appeal process.

### 2. Mental Health App Gamification

A wellness startup in 2024 requires users to log mood data via a smartphone app to qualify for corporate wellness bonuses and insurance discounts. Users are disciplined by constant notifications and a points system that rewards 'positive' entries while penalizing reports of severe distress to avoid flagging the user as 'non-compliant.' The company aggregates this data to sell predictive behavioral profiles to insurers, effectively governing employee conduct at a distance through the promise of health. This creates a 'confessing' subject who constantly self-monitor and curates their internal state for an external gaze.

**What foucault sees:** Governmentality shapes conduct through the quantification of the self, turning intimate psychological experiences into measurable data points that regulate behavior and optimize corporate productivity.

**What foucault misses:** The genuine therapeutic value some users find in sharing their struggles with a supportive, albeit monitored, peer community within the app.

### 3. University Credit-by-Exam Standardization

In 2025, a state university replaced traditional essay finals with standardized digital assessments generated by LLMs to ensure uniform grading metrics across diverse campus departments. Students are now disciplined by the need to optimize their answers for a specific, narrow definition of 'critical thinking' that the system recognizes as valid. Faculty who challenge these metrics or advocate for alternative evaluation methods are sidelined as 'inefficient,' while the institution produces graduates who are fluent in the specific language of assessment but alienated from deep, unmeasured inquiry.

**What foucault sees:** The educational apparatus functions as a machine of normalization that produces docile intellectuals by restricting what is sayable and classifiable as 'knowledge' to only what fits the algorithmic grid.

**What foucault misses:** The frustration of students who feel their unique cultural perspectives and non-standardized modes of reasoning are being invalidated by a rigid, bureaucratic framework.
