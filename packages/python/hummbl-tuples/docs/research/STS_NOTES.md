## Question

What does Science and Technology Studies add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

STS gives HUMMBL the sociotechnical lens it needs: systems are not just algorithms or organizations, but co-produced arrangements of knowledge, power, infrastructure, classification, and practice. That makes STS one of the best domains for understanding why a technically strong reasoning system can still fail, distort, or exclude in the real world.

For HUMMBL, the biggest lesson is that tuples and Base120/BaseN will not live in a vacuum. They will be embedded in institutions, categories, standards, and infrastructures that shape what counts as valid reasoning and who gets to use it.

## Core Ideas

- science and technology are co-produced with social order
- infrastructure is relational, not just technical
- classification systems organize work, visibility, and power
- standards and categories can silence some worlds while making others legible
- human and nonhuman actors jointly make sociotechnical systems work

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- treat classification as a governance act, not a neutral labeling exercise
- design tuples as infrastructural objects that mediate work, not just logs
- acknowledge co-production: the system and the social order around it shape each other
- build for heterogeneous actors, including humans, software, standards, and institutions
- make the politics of categories visible instead of burying them in implementation

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- assuming technical design can be separated cleanly from social order
- treating classifications as natural or value-neutral
- building infrastructures that make some stakeholders invisible
- ignoring the work that standards, metadata, and categories do
- treating governance as an add-on to a neutral system

## Relation To HUMMBL

- Base120 / BaseN  
  Should be treated as a sociotechnical classification and reasoning system, not a pure internal taxonomy.
- tuples / governance  
  Very strong fit. Tuples can encode categories, standards, provenance, responsibilities, and infrastructural work.
- world models / reasoning traces  
  STS keeps HUMMBL honest about whose world is being modeled and whose practices make the traces meaningful.
- readiness / BKI  
  Extremely strong fit. BKI is the sociotechnical layer where shared categories, legitimacy, and interpretive infrastructure live.
