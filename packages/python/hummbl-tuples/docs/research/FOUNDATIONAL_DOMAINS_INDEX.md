## Why These Domains Matter

- Cognitive science gives the architecture vocabulary: mental models, bounded rationality, analogy, and distributed cognition.
- Organizational theory gives the coordination vocabulary: routines, authority, institutions, sensemaking, and organizational learning.
- HCI gives the operator vocabulary: mixed initiative, explainability, usable control surfaces, and human-AI collaboration.
- CSCW gives the coordination vocabulary: shared artifacts, awareness, handoffs, and collaborative work protocols.
- Design theory gives the framing vocabulary: wicked problems, reflective practice, reframing, and iterative problem structuring.
- Implementation science gives the rollout vocabulary: readiness, fit, fidelity, adoption, and sustainability.
- Collective intelligence gives the aggregation vocabulary: diversity, group reasoning, shared memory, and coordination protocols.
- Learning sciences gives the adaptation vocabulary: scaffolding, metacognition, feedback, and transfer.
- Cybernetics gives the control vocabulary: feedback, regulation, variety, and viability under disturbance.
- Knowledge management gives the memory vocabulary: tacit and explicit knowledge, organizational memory, retrieval, and reuse.
- Systems engineering gives the lifecycle vocabulary: requirements, interfaces, integration, verification, and validation.
- Operations research gives the tradeoff vocabulary: constraints, objectives, sensitivity, and allocation under uncertainty.
- Safety science gives the risk vocabulary: accidents, safeguards, systemic failure, and high reliability.
- Resilience engineering gives the adaptation vocabulary: graceful degradation, monitoring, recovery, and learning under disturbance.
- Incident learning gives the postmortem vocabulary: blameless review, recurrence prevention, and operational memory.
- Scaling laws give the growth vocabulary: tradeoffs among parameters, data, compute, and post-training effort.
- Mathematical foundations give the theory vocabulary: approximation, optimization, uncertainty, invariance, and dynamics.
- Wissner-Gross and PSI give the AI-for-physics vocabulary: scientific reasoning, simulation, verification, and infrastructure for discovery.
- STS gives the sociotechnical vocabulary: co-production, infrastructure, classification, and power.
- Sociology of knowledge gives the legitimacy vocabulary: shared categories, credibility, expertise, and social knowledge formation.
- Consciousness studies give the introspection vocabulary: self-modeling, broadcast, attention, and caution about overclaiming.
- Epistemology gives the knowledge vocabulary: justification, reliability, testimony, disagreement, and epistemic humility.
- Philosophy of science gives the model-and-evidence vocabulary: explanation, idealization, scope, and standards of support.

## HUMMBL Framing

- `Base120/BaseN` should be read as a reasoning and interpretation layer, not a complete theory of mind.
- `tuples/governance` should record not only outcomes but also representations, interventions, evidence, routines, and operator choices.
- `world models/reasoning traces` should stay distinct from mental models and from human-facing explanations.
- `readiness/BKI` should be informed by cognition, interface design, organizational coordination, and knowledge-quality constraints, not only by task output quality.
