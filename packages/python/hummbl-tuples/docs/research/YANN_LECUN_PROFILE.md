## Question

Why does Yann LeCun matter for HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

LeCun is most useful to HUMMBL as a corrective against language-only thinking. His line of work keeps pressing the same point: useful intelligence needs predictive structure, abstract latent representations, uncertainty over futures, and planning over consequences.

For HUMMBL, that supports a sharper distinction between mental-model operators and machine-side world models. Base120 or BaseN should not be framed as world models by themselves; they are better understood as governed reasoning operators acting over partial world state.

## Core Ideas

- world models matter more than next-token imitation for robust intelligence
- self-supervised learning should produce predictive abstract representations
- planning and action require latent predictive structure, not only text fluency
- useful intelligence needs multiple plausible futures, not one fluent continuation
- current LLMs are useful but insufficient as a path to human-level intelligence

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- keep a strong distinction between reasoning vocabulary and predictive world state
- treat predictive latent structure as a separate layer from explicit reasoning traces
- preserve multiple-hypothesis reasoning over branching futures
- use LLMs for interface and synthesis while building richer planning and state layers over time

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- claiming HUMMBL itself is already a world model
- collapsing mental models into predictive state representations
- overcommitting to anti-LLM rhetoric when the practical system still benefits from LLM reasoning
- assuming physical-world modeling results transfer cleanly into organizational and social settings

## Relation To HUMMBL

- Base120 / BaseN  
  LeCun supports treating Base120 and BaseN as reasoning-operator systems, not full world models.
- tuples / governance  
  Tuples can govern which operators were used, what evidence grounded them, and how paths were chosen.
- world models / reasoning traces  
  World models represent state and dynamics; reasoning traces show how the system reasoned over that state.
- readiness / BKI / mission-governed operation  
  LeCun is less directly useful on social coordination, but his architecture instincts help keep HUMMBL's technical claims disciplined.
