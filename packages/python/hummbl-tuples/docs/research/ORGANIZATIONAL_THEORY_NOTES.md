# Organizational Theory Notes

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

What does organizational theory add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Organizational theory gives HUMMBL a disciplined way to think about coordination, routines, authority, institutions, and organizational learning. It shifts the frame from what a single smart agent can do to how work actually gets done across roles, rules, memory, and incentives.

For HUMMBL, this matters because Base120/BaseN and tuple-governed reasoning will fail if they are treated only as individual cognition tools. They also need to fit routines, authority structures, and learning processes inside real organizations.

## Core Ideas

- organizations coordinate through routines, roles, and authority structures
- organizational learning is not the same as individual learning
- institutions shape what actions are seen as legitimate or appropriate
- routines can create both stability and change
- organizations process information under constraints, not with omniscient rationality

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- treat routines as first-class design objects
- model authority, roles, and decision rights explicitly
- distinguish organizational memory from individual memory
- expect adaptation and drift, not only fidelity
- use institutional fit and legitimacy as part of deployment thinking

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- designing only for individual genius workflows
- assuming local optimization will automatically produce organizational effectiveness
- treating routines as static bureaucracy instead of dynamic coordination structures
- ignoring legitimacy, authority, and role boundaries
- collapsing organizational learning into prompt history or model fine-tuning

## Relation To HUMMBL

- Base120 / BaseN  
  Should be usable inside routines, roles, and organizational learning processes rather than only inside individual reasoning sessions.
- tuples / governance  
  Very strong fit. Tuples can encode routines, handoffs, authority, role obligations, and institutional constraints.
- world models / reasoning traces  
  Organizational theory helps distinguish what the organization knows, what the agent infers, and what the official routine permits.
- readiness / BKI  
  Extremely strong fit. Readiness depends on authority, routines, learning climate, and institutional legitimacy, while BKI depends on shared interpretive infrastructure.

## Confidence

High on relevance; medium-high on the exact HUMMBL synthesis because it integrates several organization-theory lines.
