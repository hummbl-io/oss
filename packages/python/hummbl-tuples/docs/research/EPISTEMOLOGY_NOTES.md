# Epistemology Notes

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

What does epistemology add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Epistemology gives HUMMBL a disciplined language for justification, reliability, testimony, disagreement, and epistemic humility. It is the domain that keeps HUMMBL from treating output quality as the same thing as knowledge quality.

For HUMMBL, epistemology is the clearest framework for deciding when a claim is warranted, when it is only plausible, and when it should be treated as provisional or contested.

## Core Ideas

- Gettier cases show that true justified belief is not enough to capture knowledge cleanly
- reliabilism shifts attention toward the reliability of the belief-producing process
- testimony is a major source of knowledge, but not all testimony deserves equal credibility
- epistemic injustice matters when credibility or interpretive resources are distributed unfairly
- disagreement should force calibration, not reflexive certainty

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- distinguish truth, justification, reliability, and confidence explicitly
- track the process that produced a claim, not only the claim itself
- treat testimony from humans and systems as a source that needs calibration
- encode disagreement and uncertainty rather than smoothing them away
- make epistemic status first-class in reasoning artifacts

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- equating a plausible answer with justified knowledge
- treating a model's confidence as a substitute for reliability
- ignoring testimonial and social sources of knowledge
- allowing epistemic injustice in operator or stakeholder input
- collapsing evidence, belief, and output into one field
