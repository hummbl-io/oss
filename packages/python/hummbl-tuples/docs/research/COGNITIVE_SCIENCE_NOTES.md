# Cognitive Science Notes

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

What does cognitive science add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Cognitive science gives HUMMBL the architecture language it needs: mental models, bounded rationality, analogy, distributed cognition, and levels of explanation. It is the main domain that prevents HUMMBL from collapsing into either pure ML or pure philosophy.

For HUMMBL, the practical lesson is that reasoning is not just output generation. It is structured cognition under limits, often distributed across people, tools, and representations.

## Core Ideas

- humans use mental models and situation-specific frames to reason under uncertainty
- rationality is bounded by time, attention, memory, and computation
- analogy is a core cognitive mechanism, not a rhetorical flourish
- cognition often depends on multiple representations and level-of-analysis discipline
- intelligence is frequently distributed across people, tools, and artifacts rather than residing only inside one mind

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- treat mental models as explicit reasoning operators rather than vague intuition
- keep levels separate: task, algorithm, and implementation
- preserve multiple representations when different tasks need different encodings
- treat analogy as a first-class mechanism for moving between Base120 or BaseN transformations
- model cognition as distributed across operators, tuples, evidence, and people

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- collapsing cognition into a single neat ontology
- treating reasoning traces as the same thing as understanding
- assuming one representation or one frame can fit all tasks
- overfitting BaseN to a static taxonomy instead of a flexible reasoning system
- ignoring organizational and tool-mediated cognition

## Relation To HUMMBL

- Base120 / BaseN  
  Best framed as a library of reasoning operators, analogical moves, and context-sensitive frames.
- tuples / governance  
  Tuples can encode frames, defaults, mappings, and decision provenance.
- world models / reasoning traces  
  Cognitive science keeps these distinct from mental models and from surface explanations.
- readiness / BKI  
  Readiness depends on whether the system can choose the right frame, representation, and stopping rule under constraint.
