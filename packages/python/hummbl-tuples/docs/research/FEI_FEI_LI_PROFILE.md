## Question

Why does Fei-Fei Li matter for HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Li matters because she pairs deep technical credibility with a persistent human-centered framing. Her ImageNet work helped catalyze modern deep learning, but her more recent public work emphasizes AI that serves people, is governed transparently, and is built with real-world context rather than abstract capability worship.

For HUMMBL, she is a useful bridge between world-centered intelligence, human-centered governance, and the practical question of how AI systems should serve people under real institutional constraints.

## Core Ideas

- large-scale data and benchmarks can reshape entire fields
- AI should be built around human needs, fairness, and public benefit
- the field needs better public investment, research access, and transparent deployment
- human-centered design is not cosmetic; it is a governance stance
- spatial/world understanding matters if AI is to interact safely with people and environments

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- keep human-centered governance as a first-class design concern
- treat data, benchmarks, and access as part of the infrastructure story
- connect reasoning systems to real institutional contexts and public benefit
- build for transparency, fairness, and accountable deployment

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- treating human-centered AI as branding only
- ignoring public access, diversity, and institutional trust
- assuming stronger models alone solve deployment and legitimacy problems
- using world-model language without human-centered constraints
