# Sociology Of Knowledge Notes

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

What does sociology of knowledge add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Sociology of knowledge gives HUMMBL a way to think about how beliefs, categories, expertise, and legitimacy are socially produced rather than merely individually held. It explains why shared interpretations, institutional authority, and social position matter to what a system treats as knowledge.

For HUMMBL, this matters because reasoning and governance are never only about truth conditions. They are also about who is believed, which categories are available, and how collective reality gets stabilized.

## Core Ideas

- knowledge is socially situated, not only privately justified
- institutions shape which beliefs are credible and which categories are available
- expertise and authority are social achievements, not just raw competence
- shared reality is maintained through externalization, objectivation, and legitimation
- social location affects credibility, interpretation, and uptake

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- treat credibility and authority as explicit parts of knowledge workflows
- encode provenance and testimonial status instead of flattening all sources
- treat category systems as social as well as technical artifacts
- make disagreement and interpretive plurality visible rather than hiding them
- use BKI as an explicitly social knowledge infrastructure, not just an information architecture

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- assuming every claim can be reduced to isolated evidence without social context
- collapsing authority, truth, and confidence into one score
- ignoring epistemic injustice or asymmetries in who gets believed
- treating governance as purely procedural while neglecting legitimacy
- assuming shared artifacts automatically produce shared understanding
