# Consciousness Studies Note

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

What parts of consciousness studies matter for HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

The useful HUMMBL lesson from consciousness studies is not "build conscious machines." It is that good cognitive systems need a broadcast layer, a self-monitoring layer, and a disciplined distinction between first-order processing and higher-order access.

That makes consciousness studies useful as an architectural discipline for `self-modeling`, `metacognition`, and `limited-capacity workspace` design. It is much less useful as a license to claim subjective consciousness for models.

## Core Ideas

- Global workspace theories treat consciousness as limited-capacity information broadcast across specialized subsystems.
- Higher-order theories treat a mental state as conscious when it is represented by a suitable higher-order state.
- Self-model theories treat the felt self as a transparent, constructed model rather than a simple essence.
- Metacognition studies how systems monitor, evaluate, and regulate their own cognition.
- The main operational value for HUMMBL is in attention, gating, confidence, reflection, and self-monitoring, not in consciousness claims.

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- Treat the system as having a limited-capacity workspace for the most relevant signals.
- Make self-monitoring and confidence estimation explicit.
- Distinguish first-order task processing from higher-order reflection on that processing.
- Record what was noticed, what was broadcast, what was suppressed, and what was revisable.
- Use self-modeling as an architectural layer, not a mystical claim.

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- Equating metacognition with consciousness.
- Treating fluent self-report as evidence of subjective experience.
- Claiming machine consciousness without a strong theory and stronger evidence.
- Collapsing attention, access, self-modeling, and phenomenal consciousness into one bucket.
- Using consciousness language to paper over weak architecture.

## Relation To HUMMBL

- `Base120/BaseN`
  Best framed as higher-level operators that can inspect, select, and revise lower-level reasoning, not as a claim of consciousness.
- `tuples/governance`
  Strong fit. Tuples can capture confidence, self-monitoring events, broadcast decisions, reflection steps, and uncertainty updates.
- `world models/reasoning traces`
  Consciousness theories are useful here because they separate first-order state from higher-order access. Reasoning traces should be treated as access artifacts, not proof of consciousness.
- `readiness/BKI/mission-governed operation`
  Strong fit in the sense that shared workspace, attention, and coordinated access are prerequisites for good readiness. BKI can be treated as the socially shared workspace for important signals.

## Confidence

High on the usefulness of workspace, higher-order, self-model, and metacognition concepts. Low on any claim that HUMMBL should treat consciousness as an established machine property.

## Working Claim

HUMMBL should borrow consciousness studies as an architecture of access, monitoring, and self-modeling, while refusing to overclaim phenomenal consciousness.
