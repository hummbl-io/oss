## Question

Why does Demis Hassabis matter for HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Hassabis matters because he ties together planning, world models, search, and scientific discovery in a way that is closer to HUMMBL's operational ambitions than pure benchmark-style ML. His work at DeepMind repeatedly shows that strong systems emerge when learning, simulation, and search are combined around hard environments.

For HUMMBL, he is the clearest bridge from model capability to mission-driven systems that can reason over state, act in environments, and support high-value discovery work.

## Core Ideas

- planning and search matter as much as raw model capacity
- world models are a route to more general action and prediction
- games and scientific problems are useful testbeds for general algorithms
- large-scale systems can learn useful structure from experience and simulation
- agentic systems should be evaluated in environments where consequences are visible
