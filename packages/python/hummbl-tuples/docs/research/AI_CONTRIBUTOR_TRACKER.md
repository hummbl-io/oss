## Working Buckets

- `world-model line`  
  contributors who sharpen predictive state, planning, simulation, and representation
- `reasoning/governance line`  
  contributors who sharpen oversight, evaluation, intervention, or control structures
- `operator/system-design line`  
  contributors who sharpen the human side of using, steering, and integrating AI systems
- `wickedness/change line`  
  contributors who sharpen systems change, coordination, or institutional adaptation

## Candidate Ranking Dimensions

- direct relevance to HUMMBL architecture
- relevance to reasoning systems
- relevance to world models
- relevance to governance or assurance
- relevance to operator experience
- publication or positioning value

## Working Rule

Do not track people only because they are famous.

Track them because their ideas help:

- sharpen HUMMBL distinctions
- improve architecture
- challenge claims
- or deepen the publication story
