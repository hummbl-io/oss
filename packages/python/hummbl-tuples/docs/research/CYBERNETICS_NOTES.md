## Question

What does cybernetics add to HUMMBL?

## Bottom Line

Cybernetics is the right umbrella for HUMMBL if you want a control vocabulary instead of just a reasoning vocabulary. The useful through-line is feedback, regulation, requisite variety, and viability under disturbance.

For HUMMBL, cybernetics explains why a reasoning system needs internal models, corrective loops, and enough diversity of responses to match the environment.

## Core Ideas

- control and communication are central, not optional
- a regulator needs enough variety to absorb the variety of disturbances it faces
- effective regulation requires a model of the system being regulated
- viable systems need to sense, coordinate, control, and adapt at multiple levels
- the observer can be part of the system being controlled

## What HUMMBL Should Borrow

- treat Base120/BaseN as a control repertoire that increases variety, not only as a taxonomy of mental models
- use tuples to encode state, disturbance, action, feedback, and recovery
- make internal models explicit for the systems HUMMBL is trying to regulate
- design for viability under change with visible sensing, coordination, correction, and escalation
- take observer effects seriously

## What HUMMBL Should Avoid

- over-centralized control that cannot absorb unexpected variety
- one-shot automation that ignores feedback and recovery
- treating explanations as if they were the same thing as regulation
- optimizing a local metric while the larger system becomes non-viable
- using cybernetic language for a static ontology instead of an adaptive loop

## Relation To HUMMBL

- Base120 / BaseN  
  Best understood as a set of control operators for selecting responses under constrained variety.
- tuples / governance  
  Very strong fit for control state, feedback, disturbances, and model assumptions.
- world models / reasoning traces  
  Cybernetics says a regulator needs a compressed model of the system; traces are telemetry unless they improve regulation.
- readiness / BKI  
  Readiness is viability under disturbance, and BKI is the shared information and control surface that makes adaptation possible.
