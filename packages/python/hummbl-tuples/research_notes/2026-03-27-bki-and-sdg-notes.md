# BKI and SDG Notes

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Summary

Two ideas look increasingly useful for the HUMMBL tuples and BaseN corpus:

- `BKI` = `Belonging as Knowledge Infrastructure`
- a refreshed `sustainability / diversity / governance` frame through the SDGs

## BKI

Working idea:

- belonging is part of the infrastructure that determines whether knowledge can move, be trusted, and support coordinated action

Why it matters for tuples:

- reasoning traces do not matter if the surrounding humans and systems cannot interpret or trust them together
- BKI may help explain:
  - override behavior
  - path acceptance
  - intervention quality
  - governance legitimacy

## SDG Lens

Useful anchors:

- sustainability: integrated social, economic, and environmental viability
- diversity: representation, inclusion, and uneven distribution of impact
- governance: institutions, accountability, authority, and partnership capacity

This can help HUMMBL avoid reducing governance to only control or compliance.

## Possible Relevance To BaseN

BaseN may eventually need to model not only:

- what reasoning operators were chosen
- who chose them
- under what control regime

But also:

- whether the surrounding knowledge environment supported coherent collective interpretation

That is where BKI may become more than a side concept.
