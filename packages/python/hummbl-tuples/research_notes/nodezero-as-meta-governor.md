# Nodezero as Meta-Governor

Date: 2026-03-27
Status: draft

## Question

Should nodezero act as the governor of BaseN reasoning experiments instead of leaving coordination entirely to local sessions?

## Initial Position

Yes, if the goal is comparable and publishable reasoning-path experiments.

## Why

Without nodezero:

- local runs can drift in profile/version assumptions
- control modes can be informal
- path comparison can become anecdotal

With nodezero:

- profile issuance is explicit
- control modes are explicit
- registry versions are pinned
- experiment assignment is explicit
- comparison can happen against a common authority

## Risk

Nodezero may over-constrain discovery and reduce beneficial local experimentation.

## Research Question

Does a centralized governor improve reasoning-path quality and reproducibility enough to justify the coordination cost?
