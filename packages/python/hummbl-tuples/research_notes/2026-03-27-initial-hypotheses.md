# Initial Hypotheses

Date: 2026-03-27

## Hypothesis 1

Typed tuple governance provides a cleaner publication surface than mixed prompts, logs, and workflow metadata.

## Hypothesis 2

`CONTRACT`, `DCT`, `DCTX`, `SYSTEM`, and `EVIDENCE` may be enough for a practical first taxonomy.

## Hypothesis 3

The strongest novelty claim is not tuple syntax. It is tuple-mediated execution governance for agent systems.

## Open Questions

- Which tuple fields are stable across implementations?
- Where does tuple vocabulary become too broad to remain falsifiable?
- Should attestation remain inside `EVIDENCE` or become its own class?
