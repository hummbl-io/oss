# Ownward Language Law Draft

**Status:** candidate draft for issue `#568`  
**Scope:** product language, copy hierarchy, and inference controls  
**Canon status:** not canonized; pending `hummbl-governance#162`  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-07-03

## Objective

Apply language discipline so product-facing and advisory copy prioritizes human capability and
command boundaries, while restricting `sovereignty` and `self-custody` to narrow, auditable use.

## Source status

Approved by Operator on 2026-06-30 and scoped from ownward product direction in issue `#568`.
This file is not a legal claim and does not change any product code.

## Core law

### Human Command language priority

- Default preferred terms: `command`, `decision authority`, `self-direction`,
  `self-authorship`, `right of refusal`, `time`, `attention`, `recovery`, `clarity`.
- Ownward should describe what is controlled by the human first, then what is delegated.

### Restricted high-weight terms

- `sovereignty` is restricted to contexts where final human authority is materially at stake and all
  operational preconditions are present (`refuse`, `revoke`, `redirect`, and `term setting`).
- `self-custody` is restricted to concrete, auditable contexts: cryptographic material, wallets,
  credentials, private keys, records custody.

### Mandatory disallow list

Do not use these as product-wide defaults:

- `sovereignty` as a synonym for empowerment, productivity, or preference.
- `self-custody` for time, attention, recovery, relationships, meaning, or general wellbeing.
- `agentic humans` to imply humans should be machine-like.

## Product-copied candidate hierarchy

1. `command`
2. `decision authority`
3. `self-direction`
4. `capability-preserving`

## Allowed wording examples

**Allowed**

- "AI that protects your command, not captures your attention."
- "My role is to keep you in command of your next decision."
- "Pause before scope expansion. Keep your authority explicit."

**Blocked unless precondition met**

- "Digital sovereignty without explicit refusal/revoke boundaries."
- "Total self-custody for user wellbeing or recovery."
- "Make humans more like AI."

## Required output checks

- At least one copy audit pass for:
  - readiness/promotion surfaces
  - scheduling/assessment pages
  - policy and onboarding text
- Copy update list records each use of `sovereignty` and `self-custody` with context.
- Any exception to default boundary requires explicit product justification.
