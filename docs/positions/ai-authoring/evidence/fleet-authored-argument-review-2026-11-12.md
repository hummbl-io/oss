## Verdict: FIX-THEN-ADOPT (as doctrine) — attacks 1642–1652 (0 FATAL · 2 WEAKEN-strong · 5 WEAKEN · 4 WEAKEN-minor)
Nothing here is dead on arrival — but the thesis sentence is the weakest part of the document, and the claims are strongest exactly where the rhetoric is quietest. The argument's real asset is that this estate *does the thing*: a standing Red gate with a 1,600-entry registry, receipted folds, preserved dissent, corrections on the record. The draft undersells that asset by claiming two words it hasn't earned ("credential," "warrants") and one property it only partially has (independence). **As internal doctrine, the repairs below make it adoptable. As public copy in its current form, it should not ship** — a sceptical reader's three easiest strikes (1642, 1643, 1648) all land on sentences the conclusion depends on.

## What needs repair (the attacks)
- **1642 (WEAKEN-strong) — independence.** Claims 1 and 8 spend independence the current architecture only partially supplies: shared substrate, shared tasking, same weights behind several rooms. Convergence of correlated passes is weak evidence, and the estate's own Baseline Practice Standard says why. Repair: state the independence conditions, grade today's fleet against them, and commit the label to increasing real independence (blind seats, cross-provider passes).
- **1643 (WEAKEN-strong) — the thesis words.** "Credential" (no issuer) and "warrants" (no coverage, no remedy) are asserted ahead of any definition of the mark — and the estate's own stale-figure history is the counterexample a sceptic will cite. Repair: define the mark (1642's criteria in 1652) or demote to "process mark / shows its work" until the definition exists.
- **1644 (WEAKEN) — receipts ≠ truth, and the audit is private.** Receipts prove the protocol ran; they documented this fleet's errors as faithfully as its truths. And a public mark resting on private stores asks for trust in an audit no reader can perform. Repair: claim process auditability as itself; require a lodged, inspectable evidence pack per marked piece.
- **1646 (WEAKEN) — specialisation vs a straw man.** The opponent is a lone model without tools or subagents; no matched comparison under the estate's own standards exists. Repair: scope the claim, print its falsifier (a pre-registered fleet-vs-single A/B), mark it operating hypothesis until run.
- **1648 (WEAKEN) — ICMJE level error + internal tension.** Journals judge per-manuscript contribution, not architectures; "solves" overclaims. And claim 3's "one agent among many" seating dilutes the singular guarantor claim 7 needs. Repair: fence to per-work, print the register distinction (agent in process / guarantor of record).
- **1649 (WEAKEN) — the missing boundary.** No pile, no single-agent winning cases — though the estate lives both (the retired mass-ping, the card storm, the wargame reserved for stakes). Repair: the boundary paragraph is the credibility paragraph; add it.
