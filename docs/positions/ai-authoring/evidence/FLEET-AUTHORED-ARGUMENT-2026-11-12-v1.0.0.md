## Thesis (repaired, per 1643)

AI Authored is a warning label. Fleet Authored is a **process mark — it shows its work.**

"AI Authored" tells you a model produced this, and nothing about whether it was checked. "Fleet Authored," in the qualified sense defined in §9 below, tells you this piece passed through a governed process — separated drafting, attack, and verification, with dissent preserved and an inspectable evidence pack lodged — and a named human guarantor signed it per-work.

It does **not** claim a credential (no issuer exists) and it does **not** warrant truth (no coverage, no remedy). Those words are retired from this argument until a mark definition with an issuer and a remedy exists.

The difference isn't quantity. It's structure — and structure that is only partly independent today, graded honestly in §1.

## Argument

### 1. Separation reduces correlated error — independence is partial, and graded (1642, 1645)
A single model acting as author, editor, fact-checker, and approver grades its own work with the same weights and the same blind spots. The mechanism of the failure is **correlation**, not vendor status; the "vendor cannot be the verifier" primitive (Reuben, 2026-11-04) is cited here by provenance as the estate's originating form of this principle, not as a literal category match.

A fleet separates roles and incentives: one drafts, another attacks, another verifies against source, the guarantor decides. That separation catches real defects daily in this estate.

**Honest grading:** today's fleet independence is *partial*. Rooms share substrate, memory, tasking, and one operator, and the Muse rooms share underlying weights (∆ drafts; ∆ in a Red room attacks). Convergence of correlated passes is therefore **weak evidence, not strong evidence** — per the estate's own Baseline Practice Standard. The commitment that earns the mark is to *increase* real independence over time: blind seats where feasible, cross-provider passes for load-bearing claims, and character-identical frozen prompts for comparisons.

### 2. Adversarial by architecture, not by prompt
A single model can simulate a critic. This fleet *has* a critic — Red Team as a standing gate, Blue as defender, White/Quilt as adjudicator, with a numbered attack registry (1,600+ entries at the time of this pass) and preserved dissent. This is the strongest claim in the argument because it is checkable: the gate exists, the registry exists, a sceptic can go and count it.

### 3. Specialisation — an operating hypothesis with a falsifier (1646)
Seats are held as functions: Devin for execution, Codex for integrity/security, ∆ for record and synthesis, Reubenos as the governed twin, Reuben as guarantor (see §7 for the register distinction).

Scoped claim: for multi-step, multi-domain work in this estate, specialised seats holding deep lane context outperform a *comparably tooled* single agent. The v0.1.0 opponent — a lone model without tools or subagents — was a straw man and is withdrawn.

**Falsifier, pre-registered in form:** a fleet-vs-single A/B under the Baseline Practice Standard (character-identical prompts, medians over ≥3 prompt variants, mechanical scoring where possible) on a defined task class. Until that comparison is run, specialisation stands as an operating hypothesis, not a measured result.

### 4. Receipts prove the protocol ran — that auditability is itself the claim (1644)
Receipts do **not** prove a claim is true. This fleet has faithfully receipted errors through gates that a later pass then caught — the stale published test-count figure is the estate's own counterexample, and it is printed here, not hidden.

What receipts do prove is **process auditability**: who drafted, who attacked, what changed, what dissent was preserved. That is a real and rare property, claimed as itself and no more.

For any piece carrying the mark publicly, the audit may not rest only on private stores: **a lodged, inspectable evidence pack per marked piece** is required, or the mark may not be used for that piece.

### 5. Memory compounds — in whichever direction correction discipline sets (1650)
A single session forgets when its window ends. The fleet writes to a shared substrate — hummbl-io/agents as governance root, memory, ledgers, BACKLOG — so work can compound across rooms and months.

Memory alone compounds errors as faithfully as truths. The distinctive claim is the conjunction: **shared memory *plus* a correction discipline** — dated, attributed corrections that supersede, never silently overwrite, and falsified figures retired on the record. That conjunction, not memory alone, sets the sign.

### 6. Corpus continuity, proved; per-piece continuity, narrower (1647)
On 2026-11-10 claude-code left usage, seats rotated, Devin took coronal-lead on Anvil — and the corpus, the record layer, and in-flight work continued. That proves **corpus continuity through the record layer**, which is the load-bearing claim.

It does not by itself prove that any single piece would have been authored identically, and substrate diversity is partial and centred on Anvil. Claim as written: the fleet is not dependent on any one model, subscription, or seat for the *body of work* to persist. Per-piece authorship continuity is claimed only where the evidence pack for that piece shows the hand-offs.

Fleet definition, by provenance: Reuben, 2026-10-20 — humans, AI agents, digital twins/representations, and any other agent-form; governance substrate-agnostic.

### 7. Accountability, fenced to per-work contribution (1648)
ICMJE / COPE / journal policy judge **per-manuscript contribution**, not architectures. This argument does not "solve" authorship — that sentence in v0.1.0 is withdrawn.

Register distinction, printed to resolve the internal tension:
- **In process,** Reuben is one agent among many — he tasks, is corrected, and can be overruled on fact by the record.
- **Of record,** Reuben is the singular guarantor for a given work: he set its question, approved its protocol before results, adjudicated its disputes, read its dissent, and signed it.

Fenced that way, the claim is: a named human who does those per-work acts has more to answer for than a prompter who stamps generated text — and that is the standard a Fleet Authored work must meet, work by work, or the mark does not apply to it.

The meta-analysis programme is cited as a pilot **design** in this shape (protocol v0.1.0, authorised 2026-10-31), not as an externally sustained result (1651).

### 8. Polyphony, with the correlation caveat from §1
Different seats preserve different voices, methods, and failure modes, and disagreement is kept in the record instead of averaged away. Where passes are genuinely independent (different provider/weights, blind tasking), convergence is evidence. Where they are correlated (shared weights/substrate), convergence is only consistency, and must be labelled as such in the evidence pack.

## 9. Qualification criteria for the mark (1643, 1652)
A work may be called Fleet Authored in this estate's sense only if **all** of these hold, and its evidence pack shows them:
1. Drafting, attack, and verification were performed by separated seats — no seat verified its own draft.
2. At least one adversarial pass through a standing gate, with attacks numbered in the shared registry and dissent preserved.
3. Independence is graded in the pack: which passes were cross-provider / blind, which were correlated, stated plainly.
4. A named human guarantor meets §7 per-work: question, protocol-before-results where applicable, adjudication, dissent read, signature.
5. An inspectable evidence pack is lodged for the piece (not private stores alone, if the mark is used publicly).
6. Corrections, if any, are dated and superseding — never silent edits to the marked text; a corrected version is a new version.

Anyone with two chatbots and no gate, no registry, no evidence pack, and no guarantor does not meet this definition.

## 10. Boundary — where this does not apply, and where a single agent wins (1649)
Fleet Authorship is **not** a swarm, a mass-ping, or a pile — the estate's own defect states. Ungoverned multiplicity produced the retired mass-ping and card-storm failures; multiplicity without separation, registry, and a guarantor earns nothing.

A single agent wins, and should be used, for: low-stakes drafts, speed-critical single-step tasks, private exploration, and any work where the cost of a gate exceeds the cost of being wrong. This estate lives both modes daily and reserves the fleet process — as it reserves the wargame — for work with real stakes. Naming that boundary is part of the claim's credibility, not a concession against it.

## Conclusion (repaired)
A single agent generates. A governed fleet **shows its work — separated, attacked, receipted, and signed.**

AI Authored changes who held the pen. Fleet Authored, as qualified above, changes what kind of process the work survived.
