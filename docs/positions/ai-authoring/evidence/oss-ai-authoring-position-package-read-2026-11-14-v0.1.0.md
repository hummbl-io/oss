The thesis is carried intact ("AI Authored is a warning label. Part III then does the package's most disciplined work: the retired words ("credential," "warrants," "solves") are named as retired *with their gate provenance* (attack 1643), so a public reader meets the fences as part of the position rather than discovering their absence. The conflict rule in Part II's preamble — any conflict resolves in favour of v1.0.0 — is the correct supremacy clause for a restatement. Blue found no strengthening, softening, or silent amendment.

**D3 — The evolution-with-receipts mechanism. PARTIALLY-DEFENDED** — the mechanism delivers three of the four things Reuben asked tracked, and its own birth entry omits the fourth's instrument. Delivered: every seeded entry (E-001–E-008) carries date, substance, authorising ruling or gate, and evidence; gate evidence is specific (attack numbers 1642–1652, the delta's 11 CLOSED · 0 OPEN, review paths); superseded text is recoverable — the retired rhetoric is quoted in E-003/E-004 (the two dead sentences named verbatim) and every superseded *version* is retained unedited with its SHA, so the old position is reconstructible at any past date, which was the design's stated test. Once published, git history supplies an external check on the ledger itself; until then, self-hashing is the only bolt the mechanism has, and it should be visibly fastened at birth. With that repair, D3 defends in full.
