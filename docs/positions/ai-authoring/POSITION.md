## Part I — AI Authoring

*The wider frame, from the estate's authorship record of 2026-10-31.*

1. **An AI system or fleet entity cannot be a listed author where accountability is required.** Under ICMJE (Apr 2025), COPE, and Nature/Science journal policies, authorship requires accountability that a non-legal entity cannot carry. This is a fence on venues, not a judgement on the work.
2. **The accountable human is author of record.** For peer-reviewed work, the human who set the question, approved the protocol before results, adjudicated disputes, and signed the work is the author of record, with the fleet's role **disclosed** — in Methods/Acknowledgements and in the cover letter. Disclosure is part of the position, not an optional courtesy.
3. **Authorship is earned, not gifted.** Reuben's standing definition (2026-10-20): the fleet includes human agents — he is an agent on the fleet — and authorship is earned under ICMJE criteria, not conferred as gift authorship.
4. **Entity-named authorship only where the author controls the surface.** On preprints and other self-controlled surfaces, the byline is the author's to set. That exception does not travel to venues whose policies fence it.
5. **The fleet, defined.** Per Reuben (2026-10-20): the fleet includes humans, AI agents, digital twins/representations, and any other agent-form; governance is substrate-agnostic. "Fleet" in this position never means a model swarm only.

*Currency:* the venue policies cited above are stated **as of November 2026** — the ICMJE recommendations as revised April 2025, COPE's position statements, and the Nature/Science portfolio policies, each as of November 2026.

**Self-binding, named and chosen:** publishing this position binds HUMMBL's own future submissions to these fences. That is a real cost — a HUMMBL submission that breached them would breach this stated position — and it is named and chosen here, not incidental.

Related instrument: the Fleet Authoring rubric, filed as PR `hummbl-io/agents#1848` (cited as a related instrument; this statement does not summarise it).

## Part II — Fleet Authoring

*The adopted doctrine, stated faithfully. Source of truth: "Fleet Authored vs AI Authored — Argument", v1.0.0, adopted as doctrine by Reuben's ruling 2026-11-12 — lodged in this package at `evidence/FLEET-AUTHORED-ARGUMENT-2026-11-12-v1.0.0.md` (SHA-256 `0a01d5b9c0dd6ce00f30e26041fa27deb192457a8ccb7ffee67359921c1224cc`). This part restates the doctrine; it does not amend it. Any conflict between this restatement and v1.0.0 resolves in favour of v1.0.0.*

**Thesis.** AI Authored is a warning label. Fleet Authored is a **process mark — it shows its work, in the qualified sense defined below: not a credential, and not a warrant of truth.** "AI Authored" tells you a model produced a piece, and nothing about whether it was checked. "Fleet Authored," in that qualified sense, tells you the piece passed through a governed process — separated drafting, attack, and verification, with dissent preserved and an inspectable evidence pack lodged — and a named human guarantor signed it per-work.

It does **not** claim a credential (no issuer exists) and it does **not** warrant truth (no coverage, no remedy). Those words are retired from this argument until a mark definition with an issuer and a remedy exists. The difference isn't quantity. It's structure — and structure that is only partly independent today, graded honestly in claim 1 below.

**The eight claims, as folded in v1.0.0:**

1. **Separation reduces correlated error — independence is partial, and graded.** The failure mechanism of a single model grading its own work is correlation, not vendor status. Role separation (one drafts, another attacks, another verifies against source, the guarantor decides) catches real defects. Honest grading: today's fleet independence is *partial* — rooms share substrate, memory, tasking, and one operator, and the Muse rooms share underlying weights (∆ drafts; ∆ in a Red room attacks) — so convergence of correlated passes is **weak evidence, not strong evidence**. The commitment is to increase real independence over time (blind seats where feasible, cross-provider passes for load-bearing claims, character-identical frozen prompts for comparisons).
2. **Adversarial by architecture, not by prompt.** A single model can simulate a critic; this fleet *has* a critic — a standing Red Team gate, Blue as defender, White/Quilt as adjudicator, a numbered attack registry, and preserved dissent. This is the strongest claim because it is checkable: the gate exists, the registry exists, a sceptic can go and count it — and for this statement the counting starts in the venue: the gate and delta reviews are lodged in this package at `evidence/fleet-authored-argument-review-2026-11-12.md` and `evidence/fleet-authored-argument-delta-v0.2.0-review-2026-11-12.md`, and the registry counts claimed here are exactly those stated in the lodged reviews (the registry itself is an estate record).
3. **Specialisation — an operating hypothesis with a falsifier.** Seats are held as functions. Scoped claim: for multi-step, multi-domain work in this estate, specialised seats holding deep lane context outperform a comparably tooled single agent. The falsifier is pre-registered in form (a fleet-vs-single A/B under the Baseline Practice Standard). Until that comparison is run, specialisation stands as an operating hypothesis, not a measured result.
4. **Receipts prove the protocol ran — that auditability is itself the claim.** Receipts do not prove a claim is true (see Part III). This fleet has faithfully receipted errors through gates that a later pass then caught — the stale published test-count figure is the estate's own counterexample, and it is printed here, not hidden. What they prove is process auditability: who drafted, who attacked, what changed, what dissent was preserved. For any piece carrying the mark publicly, the audit may not rest only on private stores: a lodged, inspectable evidence pack per marked piece is required, or the mark may not be used for that piece.
5. **Memory compounds — in whichever direction correction discipline sets.** The distinctive claim is the conjunction: shared memory *plus* a correction discipline — dated, attributed corrections that supersede, never silently overwrite, and falsified figures retired on the record. Memory alone compounds errors as faithfully as truths.
6. **Corpus continuity, proved; per-piece continuity, narrower.** The fleet is not dependent on any one model, subscription, or seat for the *body of work* to persist — proved on 2026-11-10, when claude-code left usage, seats rotated, and Devin took coronal-lead on Anvil, and the corpus, the record layer, and in-flight work continued. That proves corpus continuity through the record layer; it does not by itself prove any single piece would have been authored identically, and substrate diversity is partial and centred on Anvil. Per-piece authorship continuity is claimed only where the piece's own evidence pack shows the hand-offs.
7. **Accountability, fenced to per-work contribution.** ICMJE / COPE / journal policy judge per-manuscript contribution, not architectures; this doctrine does not "solve" authorship. Register distinction: *in process*, Reuben is one agent among many — he tasks, is corrected, and can be overruled on fact by the record; *of record*, he is the singular guarantor for a given work — he set its question, approved its protocol before results, adjudicated its disputes, read its dissent, and signed it.
8. **Polyphony, with the correlation caveat.** Different seats preserve different voices, methods, and failure modes, and disagreement is kept in the record instead of averaged away. Where passes are genuinely independent, convergence is evidence; where they are correlated, convergence is only consistency, and must be labelled as such in the evidence pack.

**Qualification criteria for the mark (§9 of the doctrine).** A work may be called Fleet Authored in this estate's sense only if **all** of these hold, and its evidence pack shows them:

1. Drafting, attack, and verification were performed by separated seats — no seat verified its own draft.
2. At least one adversarial pass through a standing gate, with attacks numbered in the shared registry and dissent preserved.
3. Independence is graded in the pack: which passes were cross-provider / blind, which were correlated, stated plainly.
4. A named human guarantor meets the per-work standard of claim 7: question, protocol-before-results where applicable, adjudication, dissent read, signature.
5. An inspectable evidence pack is lodged for the piece (not private stores alone, if the mark is used publicly).
6. Corrections, if any, are dated and superseding — never silent edits to the marked text; a corrected version is a new version.

Anyone with two chatbots and no gate, no registry, no evidence pack, and no guarantor does not meet this definition.

**Boundary (§10 of the doctrine).** Fleet Authorship is not a swarm, a mass-ping, or a pile — the estate's own defect states. Multiplicity without separation, registry, and a guarantor earns nothing. A single agent wins, and should be used, for: low-stakes drafts, speed-critical single-step tasks, private exploration, and any work where the cost of a gate exceeds the cost of being wrong. The fleet process is reserved — as the wargame is reserved — for work with real stakes. Naming that boundary is part of the claim's credibility, not a concession against it.

## Part III — What we do not claim

- **Not a credential.** No issuer exists. The word "credential" was retired from this doctrine at the gate (attack 1643) and stays retired until a mark definition with an issuer and a remedy exists.
- **Not a warrant of truth.** No coverage, no remedy; the word "warrants" was likewise retired at the gate. Receipts prove the process ran — they do not prove the conclusions are true. This fleet has faithfully receipted errors through gates that a later pass then caught; the doctrine prints that counterexample rather than hiding it.
- **Independence is never assumed from fleet size.** Independence is per-piece, graded under §9 criterion 3. A large fleet of correlated passes is consistency, not corroboration.
- **Not a solution to authorship.** The doctrine does not "solve" authorship (that sentence in the doctrine's v0.1.0 draft was withdrawn at the gate); it fences accountability to per-work contribution, as in Part II, claim 7.
- **Public use, ruled.** The doctrine was adopted for internal use on 2026-11-12, with public use expressly reserved. That ruling was given by Reuben Bowlby on 2026-11-14, after the public-use gate discharged (attacks 1732–1737, 6 CLOSED); this statement is placed publicly under it. The reservation's history is E-007 and E-011 in EVOLUTION.md.
