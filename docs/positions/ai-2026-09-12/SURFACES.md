## Services process wording

GOV-004 records a wording inconsistency: the services process description
presents receipts as proof of events and authority, while later sections state
integration and identity limits. Proposed replacement for that process item:

> Preserve evidence of mediated actions within the declared trust domain.
> Receipt checks support review of the recorded action and authority context;
> integration, identity and observation controls determine what can be established.

The published page remains evidence of its original wording until the replacement
is actually deployed and checked. This proposal is not a deployment receipt.

## Discovery limits

The corpus covers the named topics and sources in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md),
including dated September release claims. It is not a census of the internet
or every company-associated social post, video, customer document or deleted page.
Sitemap and some domain retrievals failed; inaccessible pages remain unresolved,
not evidence of absence. Liked activity is not automatically authored company copy.

For a new surface, record the public URL, publication/access dates, bounded
wording, affected claim ID, supporting or contrary evidence, and disposition.
Keep retrieval gaps explicit. Verify the changed public page before reporting
that an editorial correction has shipped.
